Privacy

What we keep, who sees it, and how to remove it.

Effective 11 November 2026. Run by Eye To Ad Media, Denver, Colorado. Contact: zach@eyetoad.com.

What you give us

When you use the form: your name, phone number, optional email, towns, move month, home size, a note about road access, the exact consent wording you accepted and when. The page you sent it from is recorded too. This site loads no ad networks, analytics or outside fonts.

Where it goes

Our team reads it first. Then up to three moving companies we have vetted receive it so that they can reach you. They run their own businesses and treat your information under their own rules. We do not sell it to anyone else.

How long

We hold requests and consent records only for as long as we need them to run the service or settle a dispute.

Control

  • Text STOP to end texts.
  • Email zach@eyetoad.com to request a copy, a correction or deletion.
  • Colorado residents have rights under the Colorado Privacy Act; write to us and we will answer.

Safety

Submissions travel over HTTPS and pass a spam filter. Please do not send anything the form does not ask for.
